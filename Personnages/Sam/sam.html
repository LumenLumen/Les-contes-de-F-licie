23 years old | 5'10"

Sam is the owner of the orphanage. She comes from a noble family from which she distances herself as much as possible. She is in a relationship with Alma, which is not accepted in Félicie.


MAGIC

Sam can distort sounds and air vibrations around her.


NOTES

  • Sam is a musician. Her favorite instrument is the lyre. She never hesitates to use her talent to lift others' spirits.
  • Sam is full of energy and good humor; she is an optimist. Sincere and confident, she is pleasant to be around.
  • Sam can fly into blind rages if pushed to her limit. In those moments, it's best not to cross her path.
  • Sam opened an orphanage to give others the chance she never had: choosing the path of a passion career.

IDEAS

  • Sam facing a pile of paperwork to fill out, looking a bit exasperated or desperate. Claire-Achlys can be with her to help.
  • Sam playing music or singing. She could be performing in front of an audience.
  • Sam playing a board game with some of the orphanage residents.

Daily outfit

Sam usually wears a floral outfit. She has been wearing a blue belt – the colour of merchants – ever since she worked at the herbalist’s shop. She has definitively abandoned the red associated with the nobility.

She always ties her hair back. Sometimes she might secure it with a clip or an accessory that’s more elaborate than a simple hair tie.
