Issæ has been living at the orphanage for three years. They grew up on the streets and harbor deep anger against those who left their family in misery.


MAGIC

Issæ is capable of creating and manipulating wind.

  • Issæ harbors a dark rage against the entire world, especially since the death of their parents.
  • Issæ has perfectly human ears under their hair. The growths on the top of their head are tufts of feathers, like an owl's.

  • Issæ tolerates Eirian's presence more easily than anyone else's.

IDEAS
