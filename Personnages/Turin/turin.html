16 years old | 5'2"

Turin has been living at the orphanage for four years. Her family does not come from Félicie, but from Nenan, a country to the north. She knows little about her homeland, which her parents fled for political reasons. Turin dreams of becoming a gemmologist.


MAGIC

Turin can make her yellow and pink spots glow to illuminate her surroundings.


NOTES

  • Often described as weird, Turin loves to surprise people and maintain a mysterious aura. She enjoys creating ambiguity and doubt.
  • Turin is very agile. She uses this talent to suddenly appear in front of people or jump into the middle of conversations. However, she is not very clumsy with her three large fingers. She is comfortable in her own body.
  • She likes to watch people and their behaviors while hidden away in a corner. She can sometimes be perceived as a bit oppressive.
  • Turin loves gathering pretty stones and things that sparkle. She collects them under the pretense of practicing for her future career as a gemmologist.
  • Turin is friends with Agathe and Vashaya.
  • She has a huge, very bushy tail.

IDEAS

  • Turin with her collection of stones or gems.
  • Turin picking a fruit from the top of a tree she climbed with agility.
  • Turin in the dark, looking sleepy, lighting her way with her magic.
