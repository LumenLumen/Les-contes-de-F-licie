12 years old | 4'0"

Haarch arrived at the orphanage shortly after Mielle, and the two of them are very close friends today.


MAGIC

Haarch does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Haarch is frail and sickly, long-term consequences of the malnutrition she suffered during childhood.
  • Haarch has no hands, which is rather disabling in daily life, so she takes great care of her wings instead.
  • Haarch is very timid and lacks self-confidence. She is afraid of others and takes refuge with the few she trusts.
  • Haarch has a very beautiful voice.
  • The feathers at the sides of her head can stand up or lay flat.

IDEAS

  • Haarch alone, singing or humming to herself.
  • Haarch flying and playing with the clouds.
  • Haarch and Mielle reviewing their lessons together while Mielle struggles; Eirian can be there to help.
