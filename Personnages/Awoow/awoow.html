7 years old | 5'3"

Awoow works at the herbalist shop as an order delivery courier and a mount! He lives with Oun and Smala. His caretaker is Kaosa.


MAGIC

Awoow does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Awoow mimics the sounds he hears very well. He often surprises people by imitating other voices.
  • Awoow mainly eats fruits and seeds.
  • Awoow is intelligent and docile. Very sociable, he needs company to be happy.
  • He is playful.

IDEAS

  • Awoow flying, loaded with the plants and remedies he has to deliver.
  • Awoow playing with Oun or Smala.
  • Awoow stealing treats from Kaosa's pocket.
  • Awoow sunbathing or preening his plumage.
