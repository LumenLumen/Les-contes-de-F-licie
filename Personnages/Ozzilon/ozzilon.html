16 years old | 5'4"

Ozzilon is an orphan who arrived in emergency housing recently and stayed. Ozzilon suffers from pyromania and antisocial personality disorder.


MAGIC

Ozzilon manipulates fire with ease. The flame at his feet is only lit when he uses his magic. He is not immune to his own flames (except on his legs).


NOTES

  • Ozzilon is pleasant and sweet at first glance. So much so that sometimes, he sounds a bit fake.
  • Ozzilon is manipulative; he likes to have control over others. He finds it difficult to take others' emotions into consideration. He doesn't do it to harm; it's more of a challenge he sets for himself.
  • He is impulsive and handles his emotions very poorly. He doesn't know how to express them, and most of the time, this translates into bursts of flames. He is a fighter.
  • He is not very academically inclined and has recently taken an interest in the world of blacksmithing. It is a bit difficult, but he has a lot of willpower and does his best.
  • Ozzilon is curious and likes to learn new things. He absolutely loves gossip.
  • Short, non-repetitive activities interest him more than others.
  • He likes to read short stories; novels require too much concentration from him.
  • Ozzilon has little ability to anticipate. He can put himself or others in danger without even realizing it.
  • The scaly parts of his design are inspired by the bearded dragon (pogona).

IDEAS

  • Ozzilon trying to read a book on the world of blacksmithing. He looks a bit lost but perseveres.
  • Ozzilon initiating a paper ball battle in class.
  • Ozzilon burning things in secret, but getting caught by a staff member at the orphanage.
