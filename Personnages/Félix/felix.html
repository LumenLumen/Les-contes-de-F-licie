Félix is a former resident of the orphanage. He now works at the herbalist shop where he was trained. He mainly prefers remedy orders.


MAGIC

Félix does not possess any particular magic other than the ability to transform into a feline.


NOTES

  • Félix is someone who is afraid of abandonment. This makes him anxious in his relationships with others. He does his best to be liked and available so that he won't be rejected.
  • Félix is anxious. He constantly puts pressure on himself and takes criticism or negative feedback very poorly.
  • Félix loves cooking and singing. He has a beautiful deep voice that he keeps to himself.
  • He is empathetic, observant, and sensitive to changes in others. He is often the first to notice when someone is doing poorly.
  • He is very kind and will try to help everyone, even if he is not comfortable with the person.
  • Félix tries to become a little braver every day, but it's a long road. He tends to admire people who seem confident.
  • Félix is in love with Thoo, but he has never found the courage to confess his feelings. Everyone knows it except Thoo.

IDEAS

  • Félix walking alone in nature to unwind and soothe his recurring stress.
  • Félix preparing plants. He is very diligent and focused on his work.
