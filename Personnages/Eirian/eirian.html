13 years old | 5'0"

Eirian has been living at the orphanage for a year. He was kicked out onto the streets by his upper-class family. He is hard of hearing.


MAGIC

Eirian does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Eirian is reserved and mostly keeps to himself. However, he is well-liked by the other teenagers, and it is not uncommon for them to come see him spontaneously.
  • Eirian is hard of hearing, but he can read lips and has a sharp mind. This helps him hide his disability, which others do not always notice, so they do not adapt to it. He is currently learning sign language.
  • He is highly intelligent, loves to learn, and has a good memory. He is considered a little prodigy by his peers. He loves reading.
  • Eirian is a jack-of-all-trades, though he tends to have hyperfixation periods where he gets completely absorbed in a specific topic, which he can talk about for hours.
  • He was always mocked and ostracized by his family because of his lack of horns. He lacks self-confidence enormously because of this.
  • He has two braids: a long one at the back of his head and a small one on the left side.
  • He has two moles under each eye. His pupils are circular, crossed by a horizontal line.
  • Eirian has a bushy tail that is very soft to the touch.
  • He is close to Issæ. He is also friends with Haarch and Mielle.

IDEAS

  • Eirian reading a book late into the night by candlelight, eventually falling asleep on top of it.
  • Eirian helping Mielle with their homework. Mielle's academic level is dreadful, and Eirian sometimes despairs.
  • Eirian talking for hours about his current hyperfixation. If he talks to Issæ about it, Issæ ignores him completely; if he talks to someone else, they might look embarrassed or annoyed depending on their personality.
  • Eirian clumsily trying to sign something to Txiki, feeling a bit intimidated by the young were-hippalectryon's stature.
  • Eirian feeling down and doubting himself, perhaps regarding his lack of horns.
  • Eirian brushing his long hair, which he seems to enjoy.

Eirian vit à l'orphelinat depuis un an. Il a été mis à la rue par sa famille bourgeoise. Il est malentendant.


MAGIE

Eirian n'a pas de magie particulière.


NOTES

  • Eirian est très intelligent, il adore apprendre et a bonne mémoire. Il est considéré comme un petit génie par ses camarades. Il aime lire.
  • Eirian est un touche-à-tout, même s'il tend à avoir des périodes où il est à fond sur un sujet précis dont il peut parler pendant des heures.
  • Eirian a toujours été moqué et mis à l'écart par sa famille à cause de son absence de cornes. Il manque énormément de confiance en lui à cause de cela.
  • Il a deux tresses : une longue derrière sa tête et une petite sur le côté gauche.
  • Il a deux grains de beauté sous chaque œil. Ses pupilles ont une forme circulaire barrée d'un trait horizontal.
  • Eirian a une queue touffue et très douce au toucher.
  • Eirian est proche de Issæ. Il est également ami avec Haarch et Mielle.

IDÉES

  • Eirian lit un livre jusqu'à pas d'heure à la lumière d'une bougie ; il finit par s'endormir dessus.
  • Eirian aide Mielle dans ses devoirs. Mielle a un niveau déplorable et Eirian désespère parfois.
  • Eirian parle pendant des heures de sa fixette du moment. S'il en parle à Issæ, celui-ci l'ignore royalement ; s'il en parle à quelqu'un d'autre, cette personne peut être gênée, agacée, etc., selon son caractère.
