16 years old | 6'11" (human) / 8'2" (equine)

Txiki has been living at the orphanage for four years and wishes to become a farmer. He is currently training under Kaosa and a market gardener.


MAGIC

Txiki can predict the weather and the direction of the wind.


NOTES

  • Txiki is strong, tall, and doesn't go unnoticed. He is well-built with broad shoulders.
  • Txiki stutters. He sometimes uses sign language with those who are comfortable with it.
  • Txiki is a werewolf-hippalectryon (were-hippalectryon). He has a bird tail and bird legs.
  • He is timid and reserved, speaking very little out of fear that people will make fun of him.
  • He is kind and helpful. He is a simple person who likes to lie down after a hard day's work, appreciating what he has accomplished. This sometimes gives the impression that he is naive or foolish.
  • Txiki is skilled at logical problem-solving and can be stubborn when he has a strong opinion on a matter (which does not happen often).
  • He loves spending time in nature and making flower bouquets. He is generally well-liked by animals.
  • His pupils are horizontal.

IDEAS

  • Txiki sowing seeds, watering sprouts, raking, or gardening. He can be drawn with dirt on his clothes or his legs.
  • Txiki having a conversation in sign language with Soren. She is the one who taught him; he was her very first student.
  • Txiki in his equine form carrying heavy loads or playing with other youths.
