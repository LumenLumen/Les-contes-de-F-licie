3 years old | 4'11"

Smala works at the herbalist shop as a remedy delivery courier! She lives with Oun and Awoow. Her caretaker is Kaosa.


MAGIC

Smala does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Smala is rather small for her species, so she escapes from her peers trying to display their superiority through her agility and flexibility.
  • Shy, she likes to have someone familiar around her, whether it is Kaosa or a well-known gryphon.
  • She has a good memory, even if things sometimes need to be explained to her for a long time.
  • Docile and sociable, she loves little attentions and cuddles.
  • Smala has a scar running across her right eye.

IDEAS

  • Smala and Awoow in the middle of a contest to see who can chirp the loudest.
  • Smala nibbling on fruits.
  • Smala curling up into a ball in the flowery grass and enjoying the summer.
