Verveine lives and works at the herbalist shop. He is in a relationship with Buster, though their relationship is not allowed in their world.


MAGIC

Verveine does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Verveine lives in symbiosis with a plant. He must adapt his clothes to leave it uncovered to the open air.
  • Verveine has been blind in one eye since birth. He has freckles. Verveine has no tail, neither in human form nor in feline form.
  • Verveine is discreet and reserved. He avoids conflicts as much as possible.
  • Verveine appears patient and calm, but he is very good at hiding his worries. Thus, he does his best to stay in his comfort zone. He aspires to live a quiet, simple life.
  • When he is comfortable, he can be cheerful or even make little jokes. He can show a particularly affectionate side, which he is usually rather ashamed of.
