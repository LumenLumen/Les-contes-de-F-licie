MAGIC

NOTES

  • She is eloquent; her speech is fluid and controlled most of the time.
  • She’s a little boss who always seems confident. She can be manipulative with others and may lack empathy. She is, however, very loyal to her friends, whom she’ll help as much as she can (sometimes using questionable methods).
  • Meliana is actually full of insecurities and doesn’t trust adults. She regularly stirs up trouble at the orphanage.

IDEAS

  • Meliana is studying. She hates it, even though she does well. She often has a carefree attitude during study sessions.
