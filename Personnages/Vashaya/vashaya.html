14 years old | 6'9"

Vashaya has been living at the orphanage for three years. She dreams of becoming a cook, just like her parents were when they were alive. She is very close friends with Agathe and Turin. She is dyscalculic.


MAGIC

Vashaya has no magic.


NOTES

  • She is a bongo centaur. The plants growing on her body are nasturtiums (Tropaeolum majus), which is a source of very strong discrimination in Chapelle d'Orée.
  • Her scars are from burns. She lost her family and her village in a fire.
  • She is cheerful, energetic, and always smiling. Very sociable and adventurous, she loves trying new experiences and meeting new friends. She can’t stand boredom and easily drags others into her adventures.
  • Vashaya also gets along very well with Txiki.
  • The patch on her nose is natural; it is neither a scar nor makeup.

IDEAS

  • Vashaya loves collecting all sorts of objects she finds on the ground. She regularly goes on treasure hunts during her walks. She usually keeps her finds (flowers, pretty pebbles, seashells...) in her bag or under her bed.
  • Vashaya loves cooking all kinds of dishes. However, due to her pyrophobia, she is very afraid whenever she has to get near a flame for cooking and may look terrified.
  • Vashaya tries to follow Turin and Agathe in their love of dance, albeit clumsily.

MAGIE

Vashaya n'a pas de magie particulière.
