17 years old | 5'10"

Milo is a young werecat who aims to become a baker. He is currently training with a local baker in town and should leave the orphanage in a few months. He is in a relationship with Azur.


MAGIC

Milo has nine lives. They are symbolized by spectral flames (they do not burn and look like will-o'-the-wisps) that he can make appear around him.


NOTES

  • Milo is a very kind person and tends to get walked all over quite easily. He is friends with Azken, Thoo, Azur, and Félix; he acts a bit like the glue of the group.
  • He is meticulous in all his tasks, even if it might take him some time. He lacks self-confidence and needs to be praised or encouraged from time to time to feel reassured.
  • Milo has slit eyes like a cat, even in his human form.
  • He was bullied during his adolescence because of his short stature (at the time) and a voice that was late to break.

IDEAS

  • Milo observing the starry night sky, pensive. He can be accompanied by Azur.
  • Milo preparing bread. This can be the stage of your choice. He wears an apron or the necessary equipment.
  • Milo playing with his magical flames.
