17 years old | 5'3"

Azken lives at the orphanage. He regularly lends a hand at the herbalist shop because he was trained there, but his dream is to sail and explore the world's oceans. He is in love with Merblanche but hasn't confessed his feelings yet.


MAGIC

Azken can manipulate water when it is in a fluid state. He cannot create matter.


NOTES

  • Azken is generally grumpy, serious, and rather unpleasant. At first glance, people don't particularly want to hang around him. Despite this, he is calm and rarely raises his voice.
  • Azken has an excellent memory and learns quickly, especially when the subject interests him.
  • He dreams of distant lands and loves drawing exotic herbals or bestiaries.
  • Azken wears a blonde ponytail; he rarely unties his hair. He has freckles on his face.
  • In his group of friends (Azur, Milo, Félix, and Thoo), he regularly argues with Thoo. Milo usually steps in to separate them.
  • Azken went through a difficult bereavement shortly after arriving at the orphanage, which led him to isolate himself a lot during his early adolescence.

IDEAS

  • Azken dreaming of travel and exploration, possibly alongside Merblanche.
  • Azken reading a sea adventure novel, completely engrossed in it.
  • Azken walking along the harbor, watching the boats and the sea.
  • Azken making or drinking tea.
  • Azken and Merblanche walking together, with Azken talking about his life or Merblanche telling him a marine legend.
  • Azur teasing Azken, asking him when he will finally confess his love to Merblanche.
  • Azken as a young teenager, sitting in his corner, pouting or drawing on the ground with a branch with a scowling face.
