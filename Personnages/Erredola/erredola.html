MAGIC

Erredola can pin down a shadow by touching it. The object or person to whom the shadow belongs will no longer be able to move if doing so changes the shape of their shadow.

For example, imagine someone standing with their shadow in front of them. They would no longer be able to move their legs because it would alter the shadow's shape, but they could still frown or stick out their tongue.


NOTES

  • Erredola is very kind, pleasant, and joyful. She is approachable and easy to befriend. She is socially at ease.

  • Serious and rigorous, she is quite cautious in professional situations. She observes, analyzes, and prevents risks as much as possible. She is a good listener.
  • She offers self-defense classes for women only. She wants to prove that women can fight too.
  • Luma can mimic sounds and speech with a certain degree of accuracy. She is seventeen years old.
  • Erredola has scars on her left hand.
  • Only the blue and green markings are makeup; the others are simply patterns on her skin.
  • For the past few months, she has been feeling insecure because she is starting to find white hairs.
