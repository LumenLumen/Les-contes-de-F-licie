MAGIC

Azur possesses great physical strength.

  • His necklace must be drawn; Azur is very attached to it.
  • Azur learned a lot about plants at the herbalist's during his stay at the orphanage.
  • His eyes are always half-closed and his pupils are vertically slit.
  • He doesn't really like his body, and he may sometimes bind his chest on days when he feels particularly uncomfortable.

IDEAS
