Grimgrim works at the herbalist shop and was the very first employee of the boutique. She alternates between preparing remedies for sale, handling customers, and treating patients.


MAGIC

Grimgrim does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Grimgrim is headstrong and won't let anyone walk all over her. She is a fighter who does what she believes is right, and she can regularly put her foot in her mouth.
  • Smiling and cheerful, she is rather pleasant to be around. She is someone who always has energy to spare.
  • Grimgrim lives in symbiosis with the plants growing on her body.
  • Grimgrim only has four digits per limb (fingers and toes).
  • Grimgrim's hair is made of seaweed. She must hydrate it regularly. When it gets very hot or dry, she can tie it up into a bun so that it preserves its moisture longer.

IDEAS

  • Grimgrim and Verveine talking; they are very close because they both suffered the same violence in the past.
  • Grimgrim reading, which is her main activity during her free time. She sometimes writes her own stories too.
  • Grimgrim preparing food. People eat a lot of fish in Félicie.
