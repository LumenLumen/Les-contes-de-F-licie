15 years old | 5'5"

Agathe lives at the orphanage. Her dream is to become a dancer. She is friends with Turin and Vashaya.


MAGIC

Agathe does not possess any particular magic.


NOTES

  • Agathe is joyful and dynamic. Determined and optimistic, she can sometimes appear candid or naive. She easily sweeps others up in her hopes.
  • Agathe loves to dance; it is her whole life. Agile and graceful, she practices whenever she can and is not afraid of being the center of attention.
  • Agathe is very upright and blindly trusts the rules and justice applied by elders. She may look down on those who do not follow the rules, yet she still sees the world with a child's eyes.
  • Agathe's design is inspired by the Swaledale sheep.

IDEAS

  • Agathe dancing — any style, she loves experimenting.
  • Agathe brushing her wool; she takes great care of it and is rather coquettish.
  • Agathe studying; even if it's not her passion, she wants to be at the top of her class.
