17 years old | 5'1"

Thoo has been living at the orphanage since she was twelve. She has been hiding among humans ever since Médien saved and took her in. She will soon have to leave the orphanage and is hesitating between returning to live as a dragon or accepting the job offer from the herbalist shop.


MAGIC

Her magic allows her to accelerate the growth of plants (she cannot create plants). Her power is useless in sterile areas. She can use it in her human form without any issues.


NOTES

  • In her dragon form, Thoo has antlers on her head and at the base of her tail.
  • Thoo is very curious; she sometimes asks indiscreet questions, especially since she did not grow up with a human filter.
  • Thoo is intelligent and understands quickly. This allows her to adapt well. However, she is very stubborn.
  • Thoo is nervous and highly suspicious of strangers. Her responses can be aggressive or abrupt. She regularly goes through periods of hypervigilance and anxiety attacks.
  • Thoo handles her emotions poorly; she is easy to read but hard to make talk. Thoo is proud, like the majority of her peers.
  • Before arriving at the orphanage, Thoo lived for a year at Médien's house back when he was still a craftsman.

IDEAS

  • Thoo observing the starry sky with sadness. She is likely reminiscing about her life as a dragon.
  • Thoo discovering a dish she has never eaten before. She enjoys human cuisine.
  • Thoo throwing a temper tantrum.
