15 years old | 4'6"

Cybèle has been living at the orphanage for two years. Rather small for her age, people often think she is younger than she is.


MAGIC

Cybèle has the power to read the thoughts of people close to her, but she cannot control it, which is rather handicapping. Overall, she perceives emotions clearly and hears the "inner voice" in people's heads. She can also perceive their subconscious mind in a blurry and distorted way.


NOTES

  • Cybèle is a werecat. Her coat patterns are not symmetrical. She has round ears.
  • Cybèle is very honest; she tends to have no filter. Rather taciturn, she is the type to observe until the last moment and panic when it's time to act.
  • Cybèle’s mood changes frequently; she absorbs her environment like a sponge because of her power. She can be moody or indecisive.
  • Cybèle tends to hide her power from others because it makes those around her uncomfortable most of the time. She tries to live like a "normal" person.
  • Cybèle often cries to release everything she absorbs.

IDEAS

  • Cybèle isolating herself from the group, overwhelmed by too many thoughts at once.
  • Cybèle running through nature, completely happy, far from others. She could be chasing a butterfly or leaping over a stream.
  • Cybèle brushing her hair or her tail.
