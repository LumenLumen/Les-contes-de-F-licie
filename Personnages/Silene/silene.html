54 years old | 1'4"

Silène lives at the herbalist shop. He is Verveine's best friend and watches over him.


MAGIC

Silène has an excellent instinct and seems to be able to communicate with all mammals. He is capable of producing an orange breath of magic that he can manipulate as he pleases.


NOTES

  • Silène is an eternal grump. Regularly in a bad mood, he doesn't mince his words and is well-known for his disparaging remarks.
  • Silène is loyal and protective, even though he rarely shows tenderness. He only shows affection towards Verveine and can be sharp-tongued with others.
  • Silène has a carnivorous diet.
  • Silène's markings and colors are not symmetrical!
  • Silène doesn't know where he comes from or if he has any family. This weighs heavily on him, and he has spent a good part of his life searching for his origins. He has traveled extensively.
  • The green extremities on his head are stylized eyebrows, not horns or antennae.

IDEAS

  • Silène hunting. Generally, he goes after small rodents.
  • Silène, with his fur bristling, getting angry at someone—possibly Buster, whom Silène doesn't like.
  • Silène in a landscape far from the herbalist shop, searching for his family. Maybe he could even ask one of your characters for directions?
