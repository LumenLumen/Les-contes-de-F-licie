7 years old | 6'6"

Oun lives at the herbalist shop and delivers remedies to the surrounding areas. He can serve as a mount.


MAGIC

Oun can cool down the air around him.


NOTES

  • Oun is very proud and loyal. Sometimes stubborn, he can also become aggressive if he is in a bad mood.
  • He is quite sensitive to flattery, and it is not difficult to coax him.
  • Oun is highly resistant to the cold.
  • Oun only has blue patterns under his wings, on his tail, and on the feathers on his head.
  • Oun is a carnivore.

IDEAS

  • Oun delivering remedies. He can be accompanied by Kaosa.
  • Oun playing with Smala and Awoow.
  • Oun hunting.
